A new home: your launcher instances, and adding a mod to one in a few clicks.

- The app opens on the instances it finds in Prism, PolyMC, Modrinth App, CurseForge,
  ATLauncher, GDLauncher, FTB App, SKlauncher and the Minecraft Launcher, each with its
  launcher's icon (or its initial), version, loader and mod count.
- Add a mod: search Modrinth for the instance's version and loader, see what is possible for
  each result (an official build, other versions only, another loader only), and add it
  straight into the instance's mods folder with its dependencies. Undo takes it back and puts
  back anything it replaced.
- History lists every add, newest first, with Undo on the ones still in place.
- Each instance says which of its mods have no build for its version and loader on Modrinth,
  each linking to its page: the first ones to look at if the game crashes.
- The engine is the `modkeel` command-line tool from PyPI, now 0.1.11.
